### 🧬 About Me

My background is in **biophysics and quantitative imaging**, where I focused on extracting meaningful signals from biological experiments using computational methods. Over time, I transitioned toward the engineering side of science: building reliable analysis pipelines, designing tools for researchers, and transforming exploratory methods into reproducible software.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **science, data, and engineering**. My work often involves computer vision, quantitative analysis, and machine learning applied to microscopy and biological data. More broadly, I am curious about how modern computational tools — from numerical modeling to AI — can help scientists explore complex datasets and accelerate discovery.

### 🛠 Core Expertise & Interests

On GitHub, I mostly build and experiment with projects related to:

*   **Scientific Computing & Data Analysis** – Turning raw experimental data into actionable insights.

*   **Research Software Engineering** – Building tools for researchers and streamlining data workflows.
